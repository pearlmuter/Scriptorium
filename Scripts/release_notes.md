**Install:** download `Scriptorium.dmg` below, open it, and drag Scriptorium into Applications. Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later. Signed and notarised.

On first use, click **Dictate** to fetch the speech model (about 2.4 GB, one time) and allow the microphone. Notes live in `~/Documents/Scriptorium` as plain Markdown.

The guardian is any LLM you connect over MCP: chat ⋯ › *Copy MCP Connection for LLM*. See the README.
